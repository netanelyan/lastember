How to play

Each run is one stretch of days and nights. Gather and hunt while it is light. When night falls, predators come for you. Each night there are more of them. See how many dawns you can reach.

Field notes

  • A full campfire burns for about 3 minutes at night. Each log adds about 20 seconds.
  • Stand next to a lit fire to warm up and to cook raw meat and fish.
  • Wolves circle outside bright firelight for a few seconds before they attack.
  • A lynx crouches before it pounces. Roll to the side.
  • Bears ignore the light and tear through wood walls. Stone walls hold about three times longer.
  • Raw food fills you a little but costs 4 health.
  • With a fishing rod, press E at the shore to cast. Reel in the moment the float dips. Now and then you haul up a crate of supplies.
  • A tent brings you back once if you fall.
  • Snow starts on night 4. From day 7 it is winter, and even the days are cold.
